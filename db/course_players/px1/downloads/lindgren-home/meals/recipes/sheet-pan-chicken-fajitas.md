# Sheet-pan chicken fajitas

Time: 30 minutes · Serves 4 · Prices are made-up estimates

## Ingredients
| Item | Store section | Est. $ |
|---|---|---|
| 1.5 lb chicken breast | Meat and Fish | 7.50 |
| 3 bell peppers | Produce | 3.00 |
| 1 onion | Produce | 0.80 |
| 8 flour tortillas | Bakery | 3.00 |
| 1 cup shredded cheese | Dairy | 2.50 |
| 1 lime | Produce | 0.40 |
| chili powder, cumin, olive oil | Pantry | 0.00 |

## How
Slice everything, toss with oil and spices, roast at 425°F for 20 minutes. Squeeze lime on top. Serve in tortillas.
