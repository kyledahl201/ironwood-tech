# Lindgren Home: house file

Claude reads this file every time it works in this folder. Keep it short and true.

## Who we are
- The Lindgrens are a made-up family for practice. Nothing here is a real person.
- Sam and Jordan (parents), Ava (11), Milo (7) and Biscuit the dog, in the made-up town of Cedar Hollow.
- We want calm Sunday nights: one plan for the week that everyone can see.

## Where things live
| Folder | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `calendar/` | The family calendar file, exported from a calendar app |
| `meals/` | What we like, what is in the pantry, and our recipes |
| `money/` | The monthly budget and each week's spending |
| `plans/` | One weekly plan per week, named by the Sunday date |
| `helpers/` | Job files for our family team |
| `hub/` | The family hub app (built in lessons 9 and 10) |

## Our family team
- Job files live in `helpers/`: ceo, cfo, coach, home, critic.
- When I say "ask the CFO" or "have the critic check this", work as that helper and follow its file exactly.
- The Family CEO runs the Sunday review and asks the others for their part.
- The Critic checks everything before I see it and never fixes its own findings.

## The Sunday review
When I say "run the Sunday review":
1. CEO: read `calendar/` and list next week, day by day, and mark busy nights. A busy night is an evening (from 4 PM on) with two or more things, or one thing that ends at 6:30 PM or later.
2. Coach: plan dinners around busy nights, with recipes from `meals/recipes/`, and one family activity.
3. Home: build the shopping list from those recipes, minus `meals/pantry.md`, grouped by store section.
4. CFO: check the grocery estimate and last week's spending against the newest `money/budget-*.csv`. If there is no budget file, skip this step and say so.
5. Critic: check the plan. Fix what it finds, then show me.
6. Save the plan as `plans/week-of-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## Never put in (this folder, Claude, or the hub)
- Passwords, PINs or security answers.
- Bank, card or account numbers. Social Security numbers.
- Medical records, diagnoses or medications. (Food rules like "no nuts" are fine.)
- Kids' full names, schools, addresses or photos. First name, activity and time only.

## Money files
- Spending files run Monday to Sunday and are named by the Monday date. Weekly plans run Sunday to Saturday.

## Calendar rules
These are instructions to Claude. The Mac's Calendars permission itself covers every calendar, so these rules matter.
- Read the Family calendar only.
- Keep first name, activity and time. Drop notes, addresses and other people's names.
- If I say "disconnect the calendar", stop reading it and tell me how to remove the permission.

## House rules
- Made-up data only in this folder.
- Ask before deleting anything.
- Plain words. If you use a technical term, explain it in one line.
- You suggest; we decide. Nothing is sent, bought or booked.
