# The Lindgren calendar (made up)

`lindgren-family.ics` is a calendar file, the kind you get from Calendar > File > Export on a Mac.

Watch for these on purpose:
- **Repeating events.** Soccer, swim and piano are written once with a repeat rule. Claude has to count them in every week they happen.
- **Notes you don't need.** Some events have notes with other people's names or places. The calendar rules say drop them.
- **Thanksgiving week.** Early release on Wednesday and a holiday on Thursday change the dinner plan.

Practice week for lessons 5 to 10: **Sunday, Nov 15 to Saturday, Nov 21, 2026.**
